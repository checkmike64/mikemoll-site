FAQ
What is this room about?
A small group of service-based founders who meet twice a month to work through whatever is on their desk that week: a price they're afraid to raise, a hire that isn't working, a delivery process AI could take over. I built it because most owners have nobody at their level to check a decision with, and the AI groups out there are full of people who haven't run anything yet. Everyone here has.
Is there a revenue minimum?
Yes, $10K a month with a stable client base. Below that, the problems are different and the room won't serve you well yet. I'd rather tell you that now than have you pay for a seat that doesn't fit.
When do you meet, and what if I can't make one?
Every second Wednesday at 1:00 p.m. Eastern, for 75 minutes. Join when you're available. The room is full of busy people.
What does a call look like?
Two or three people bring a decision they're stuck on, the rest of us ask questions until the problem is clear, then everyone who has been through something similar says what they did and how it went. People also share what they're working on, or break down something they've discovered for the benefit of the group.
How much do I need to know about AI?
Not much. You should already be using it to run parts of your business, but plenty of members aren't technical and taught themselves what they needed. What matters is that you reach for it before you reach for busywork.
