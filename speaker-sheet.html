About

After building 150+ offers, Mike has found that 90% of the problems service founders face are due to the offer.

It's usually one of four problems:

  1. Positioning that doesn't attract ideal clients
  2. No unique factor to stand out in the AI era
  3. Delivery that's bloated and hard to scale
  4. Pricing set to win contracts, with no room to grow

Mike helps founders find the one holding them back most and fix it first.
