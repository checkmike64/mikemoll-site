Why podcasts

Ads and posts stop working the day you stop.

A boosted post is gone by the weekend. A podcast appearance still gets found a year later, ranking in search and turning up in someone's feed. Guesting takes work to land. The difference is the work keeps paying after you stop.

The compounding channel

Why one good interview beats a month of posting.

Authority transfer

The host spent years earning their audience's trust. In one sentence, some of it becomes yours. On other channels that takes years.

Undivided attention

Listeners finish 80% of an episode. That's 30 to 60 minutes of full attention, not a three-second scroll. A post gets glanced at. An interview is a conversation.

Permanence

Episodes keep working for years. They rank in search and bring people to you long after you've moved on. Every appearance is a brick in your wall of credibility.
