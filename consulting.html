You make good money, but it's wearing you out.

You're good at what you do, and your clients get results because of it. But most of that work can only be done by you, so the whole business runs on you and never slows down. Here's the cycle:

Price low to win it → Say yes to keep moving → Do all the work yourself → No time to build or market

You have no time to work on the business because you're drowning in it. It shows up in three places.

Constraint 1

Offer & belief

You write the number, then you talk yourself down and send a smaller one. You price on what the client can afford, not on the value you create, because you can't afford to lose the deal. You've meant to raise your rates for a while, but you don't fully believe the higher number yet, so it never comes out of your mouth.

Constraint 2

Sales

You don't have a sales process, so a good call ends and you can't tell if it's a yes. You couldn't say what made it land, so you can't do it on purpose next time. When the silence runs long, you drop the price before they push back. Playing small shows, so even the clients who sign walk away a little unsure they picked right.

Constraint 3

Delivery

The scope drifts a little each week, and you take the unpaid extra because saying no is harder. It all runs through you. You hand work to a contractor and it comes back wrong, because there's no process to give them. You've thought about hiring and backed off, so the systems never get built. You wanted freedom, and instead the business runs on you.
