# Hero: separating "client results" from "Mike's background"

You flagged that the two bubble rows blur together, especially on mobile, and the background pills feel like they're trying to be proof but don't quite land. I asked four coaches (Gardner, Laja, Wiebe, Wolf). They agreed almost completely. Here's the recommendation.

---

## The core problem (all four agreed)

The two rows do **two different jobs**, but they're wearing the **same costume** (identical pills, same row), so the reader can't tell them apart:

- **Results** answer *"will this work for someone like me?"* (proof of outcome)
- **Background** answers *"can I trust this person?"* (proof of competence)

Same visual = reads as the same message. On mobile they stack into one undifferentiated column and the distinction dies.

---

## The fix (four moves)

**1. Label each group with a tiny header.** This is the single highest-impact change and it's what kills the mobile blur. A small uppercase mini-header above each cluster tells the reader what they're looking at before they read a pill.

- Results label options: **"Real client results"** (clear) · "Clients like you" (Wolf's, more emotional) · "What clients get"
- Background label options: **"Who's coaching you"** (Wiebe's, my pick) · "Why trust me" · "My background"

**2. Make the two look different — demote the background.** Right now both are pills. Gardner's move (I agree): keep **results as the loud, colored pills** (they've earned it, they're specific numbers), and turn **background into one quiet line of muted text** with dot separators, not badges. Text reads as "bio," pills read as "trophies." That contrast alone does most of the separating.

**3. Results first, background second — unanimous.** The anxious buyer wants to know it works for people like them *before* they care who you are. Outcome earns attention; credentials close the trust gap right after.

**4. Cut it from five pills to four (2 + 2).** Five items under the CTA is too much load, and one background pill is the weak link:

- Keep: **"Fractional CMO, $20M company"** and **"1,000+ sales calls reviewed"** (both directly relevant to a coaching buyer).
- Cut or move to the bio: **"Digital Lead · Volvo & Patak's."** Three coaches flagged it — Volvo and Patak's are mismatched in size, it reads as name-dropping, and brand logos don't prove you can grow a coaching client's revenue. (If you want to keep one, Wiebe says tighten to a single logo, e.g. "Led digital for Volvo.")

---

## What I'd ship

```
Real client results
  [ Tyler tripled his retainer: $1,500 → $4,500 ]   [ Sean: $16K → $30K a month, fewer hours ]

           ( generous gap )

Who's coaching you
  Fractional CMO, $20M company  ·  1,000+ sales calls reviewed
        (muted text line, not pills)
```

Results = colored pills, loud. Background = one calm line of text, quiet. Two labels doing the pointing. On mobile it stacks as: label → 2 pills → gap → label → 1 text line. No more blur.

---

## Optional upgrade (Wolf's note)

The résumé items risk sounding like a flex against your calm, peer tone. The fix isn't to cut them, it's to spend the authority *on the buyer*:

- Instead of: "1,000+ sales calls reviewed"
- Try: **"1,000+ sales calls reviewed, so you skip the mistakes I've already seen."**

Authority framed as "here's what that means for you" builds trust; authority paraded builds distance. Worth it for at least the strongest credential.
