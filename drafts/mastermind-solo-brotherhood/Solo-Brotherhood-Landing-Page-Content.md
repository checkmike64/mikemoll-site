Here, someone's already built the automation you were about to lose a weekend to, and they'll hand it over. You skip the dead ends they already paid for.

## WHAT THIS IS

Think of it as a board of advisors who don't take equity or a salary.

You stop building alone. Most meetings are somebody's real problem: a price they're scared to raise, a hire they can't get right, an offer that won't close. The first question in the room usually isn't "what would you do." It's "what did you build or automate so you never have to touch this again."
