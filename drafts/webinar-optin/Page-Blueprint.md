# Webinar Opt-In Page — Blueprint

**Training:** "How to Get Booked on Podcasts" (free, live) · Host: Mike Moll
**One goal:** register (email opt-in via GHL form). No pricing, no upsells, no nav/links. Every CTA scrolls to `#register`.
**Approach:** lead with the payoff (authority, network, revenue), prove it, then hand over the plan. Lean copy, let layout carry it. Book-powered, Mike's voice.

## 2. Why now (the shift)
**Purpose:** why this channel, why it matters, right now.
- Headline: **Every other channel makes you rent attention. The rent keeps going up.**
- Body (tight): *Content got industrialized. LinkedIn is a flood, inboxes overflow, and the moment you stop paying (in money or time) you lose ground. Podcast guesting works differently. It ignores follower counts, can't be gamed at scale, and it compounds.*
**Layout:** short centered statement band, maybe a small "rented vs. owned" two-chip contrast. Keep to ~2 short lines.

## 3. What guesting actually does for you — the three benefits
**Purpose:** the core benefit engine (straight from the book).
- Section headline: **Why one good interview beats a month of posting.**
- Three cards (color-coded sky / navy / terracotta):
  1. **Authority transfer** — The host spent years earning their audience's trust. In one sentence, some of it becomes yours. A level-up that takes years on any other channel.
  2. **Undivided attention** — Listeners finish 80% of an episode. That's 30–60 minutes of full attention, not a 3-second scroll. A post gets glanced at; an interview is a conversation.
  3. **Permanence** — Episodes keep working for years. They rank in search and bring people to you while you've moved on. Every appearance is a brick in your wall of credibility.
**Layout:** 3-card grid, icon + short headline + 1–2 lines each.

## 4. The payoff, in numbers ($386K)
**Purpose:** make the outcome concrete and credible.
- Big stat: **$386,000** traced to podcast appearances.
- Breakdown (three small stats): **70%** from hosts (referrals, intros, partnerships) · **15%** from listeners · **15%** from repurposed content.
- One-line proof: *One interview led to an investment firm reaching out. That became $150,000 of work across three brands. No pitch. I showed up and said what I believed.*
- Anchor line: **The podcast is the introduction. The relationship is the business.**
**Layout:** deck-style stat band (big number) + 3 small stat tiles + one short story line.

## 5. You don't need what you think you need
**Purpose:** clear the objection (small audience / no story) without making it the lead.
- Headline: **You don't need a big following or a dramatic story.**
- Body: *You need a clear point of view, honest experience, and the willingness to share it. You're one step ahead of someone who needs exactly what you know. That's enough.*
- Optional line: *Built for consultants, coaches, agencies, service owners, authors, and speakers tired of marketing that doesn't compound.*
**Layout:** calm reassurance block, centered. Short.

## 6. What you'll learn in the training
**Purpose:** the plan — what the free session teaches.
- Headline: **In this free training, you'll learn how to get booked.**
- Three items:
  1. **Make your story sellable** — frame your experience so the right host says yes.
  2. **Build assets that get a yes** — the bio, speaker sheet, and titles a host needs.
  3. **Get the outreach right** — stand out in a full inbox and win the host over.
- Close line: *Plus how to turn a single appearance into revenue and reusable content.*
- CTA: **Save My Seat** → `#register`
**Layout:** 3 numbered blocks + button.
