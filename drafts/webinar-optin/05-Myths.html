You might be thinking…

  • I need a big audience
  • I need a big social media following
  • I need a crazy story
  • I need a big company exit

None of these is what gets you booked. The host brings the audience. What they want is a guest with a clear point of view and something useful to say. If you've written a book or built expertise people respect, you already have that. You're more bookable than you think. You just need the system.
