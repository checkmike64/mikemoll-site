TLDR

  • You can fill a pipeline with good leads without spending on ads.
  • Personalized video, referrals, and podcast guesting get you in front of buyers.
  • Quality beats volume when the leads already trust you.

Mike joined Donald C. Kelly on The Sales Evangelist to talk about a question every seller has. How do you get more high-quality leads when you are not going to buy your way in with ads.

Mike's answer is not a growth hack. It is a small set of habits that put you in front of the right buyers with trust already built in.

Ads are not the only door

Ads can work, but they are not the only way, and for a lot of experts they are the wrong first move. Mike leans on organic reach, referrals, personalized video, and podcast guesting to get in front of buyers. The point of each one is the same. Show up where your buyer already is, be useful, and let trust do the selling. Leads that come in warm close faster and haggle less than leads you rented from an ad platform.

Personalized video is the quiet workhorse

Mike's favorite low-cost tactic is the short personalized video. Forty seconds to a minute and change, made for one person. Be friendly, disarm them, show you did your homework, give one clear offer, give one reason to say yes, then ask. It works because the buyer can see you paid attention. That is rare, and rare gets replies. It is trust at the very first touch, which is exactly what a cold ad can never buy.

Quality comes from fit, not volume

More leads is not the goal. Better leads is. Mike's whole approach to sales is that people buy when they feel understood, not when they understand you. So the aim is to attract people whose problem you can clearly name and solve, then vet them for fit before saying yes. That keeps your pipeline full of the right people instead of clogged with the wrong ones.
