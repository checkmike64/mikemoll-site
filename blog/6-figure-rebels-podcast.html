TLDR

  • Podcast guesting puts you in front of the right buyers with trust built in.
  • The goal is better-fit clients, not just more downloads.
  • Show up useful, name the problem you solve, and let fit do the sorting.

Mike joined Jaime Atkinson on the 6 Figure Rebels Podcast to talk about using a show to bring in clients. Mike has been a guest on more than seventy podcasts, so this is a channel he knows well.

Here is how he thinks about turning a show into clients.

A podcast is a trust machine

Ads rent you attention for a moment. A podcast earns you trust for an hour. When a good-fit buyer hears you talk through their problem for a full episode, you show up already understood, not as a stranger in their feed. Mike leans on guesting and organic reach for exactly this reason. Leads that arrive warm close faster and haggle less than leads you paid to interrupt.

Fit beats volume

The trap with a show is chasing download numbers. Mike's view is that better clients matter more than bigger audiences. People buy when they feel understood, so the aim is to name the exact problem you solve and let the right people lean in while the wrong ones tune out. Then vet for fit before you say yes. A smaller show full of the right listeners beats a big one full of the wrong ones.

Make the client flow repeatable

A show only works as a channel if it does not live and die on your energy each week. Mike's fix is the same one he uses everywhere. Record the steps, bucket the tasks, and hand off what someone else can carry, so the booking and follow-up keep running. That is what turns guesting from a nice bump into a steady source of clients. For more, the Sproutworth conversation covers making the flow predictable, and the Hailey Rowe episode digs into lead gen for coaches.
