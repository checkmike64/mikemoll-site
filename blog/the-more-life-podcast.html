TLDR

  • You do not need a big budget to bring in good leads. You need a few simple channels you can keep up.
  • Lean marketing means organic content, referrals, and personalized video instead of paid ads.
  • The best marketing feels like being useful, not like shouting.

The More Life Podcast is about squeezing more out of life and finding meaning in your work. Host Bart Anestin comes from the creative world, so this was less about tactics for their own sake and more about building a business that fits the life you want.

I talked about generating leads with lean marketing. The goal is a handful of simple channels you can run without burning out or spending money you do not have.

What lean marketing looks like

Lean marketing is simple and low cost on purpose. Organic content, referrals from happy clients, and short personalized video that builds trust early. A forty second to a minute video sent to the right person does more than a month of cold blasts.

You do not need every channel. You need the two or three that fit how you like to work, done consistently. Most people spread themselves thin and wonder why nothing lands.

Marketing that feels like being useful

The mindset matters as much as the method. If marketing feels gross to you, you will avoid it. So flip it. Marketing is showing the right people that you understand their problem. People buy when they feel understood, not when they understand you.

When you frame it that way, the work fits the kind of business a creative person wants to run. You are helping first, and the leads follow. I dug into this way of thinking on the InnovaBuzz podcast and on the Hailey Rowe podcast.
