TLDR

  • You do not need a big ad budget to land good clients.
  • A short, personalized video builds trust at the first touch.
  • Marketing works better when it is built around helping, not shouting.

Mike joined Josh Elledge on The Thoughtful Entrepreneur to talk about lean, impact-driven marketing. The theme was simple. How do you grow when you would rather help people than blast them with ads.

Mike's answer leans on the tools most founders already have. Your face, your voice, and a little bit of homework about the person you want to reach.

Lean beats loud

You do not need a huge budget to get in front of the right people. Mike's whole approach is built for founders who want to stay lean. Organic reach, referrals, and personalized video get you in front of buyers without paying for every click. The goal is not to be everywhere. It is to be useful to the handful of people who are a fit for what you do.

Personalized video as a first touch

Here is the tactic Mike keeps coming back to. Record a short native social video, around forty seconds to a minute and change, made for one person. Be friendly. Disarm them. Show that you did your homework on them. Give one clear line about what you do and one reason to say yes. Then ask, do not tell. That short clip builds trust faster than a wall of text because the person can see you are a human who paid attention. It is lead generation that feels like a favor, not a pitch.
