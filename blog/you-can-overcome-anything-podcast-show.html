TLDR

  • I quit a job I hated with no plan and no degree, then had to figure the rest out.
  • The turnaround came from three things: better pricing, tighter process, and better client fit.
  • The obstacle was not a lack of talent. It was believing my work was worth charging for.

You Can Overcome Anything, hosted by Cesar R. Espino, is a show about people who came through hard things and kept going. So this one was personal. I told the story of how I got here, including the parts that were not pretty.

I quit a job I could not stand with no plan and no degree to fall back on. What came after was years of figuring it out, including a stretch that got close to bankruptcy.

The part where it nearly fell apart

I spent thirteen years running a Google Ads agency. From the outside it looked fine. On the inside I was close to burnout, the business was shrinking, and I was doing more work for less money than ever.

The hard truth was that I had built something that depended entirely on me grinding. When that runs out, so does the business. I had to stop and rethink the whole thing instead of just working harder.

How I rebuilt

The turnaround came down to three things. I fixed my pricing, because I had been undercharging by about half while doing more work than I was paid for. I tightened my process so the work was repeatable instead of reinvented every time. And I got picky about client fit, because the wrong clients drain you faster than anything.

Underneath all of it was a belief problem. Belief comes before price. Once I believed my work was worth charging for, the pricing and the boundaries followed. Now I coach experts to make themselves easier to sell. I got into the money and mindset side of this on the Hailey Rowe podcast and Fox Talks Business.
