TLDR

  • You stand out by being clear about who you help, not by being loud.
  • One sharp offer beats a long list of what you can do.
  • Trust built early is what makes a buyer choose you.

Mike joined Christopher Dedeyan on The Peak Performance Greatness Show to talk about how to stand out effectively. Not by shouting louder. By being clearer.

Here is how Mike thinks about getting noticed by the right people.

Clarity is how you stand out

Most experts try to stand out by adding more. More services, more claims, more noise. It backfires. The buyer gets confused and moves on. Mike's take is the opposite. Point your offer at one clear outcome for one clear buyer. When you are that specific, the right person knows in seconds that you are for them. Clear beats clever, and clear is what gets remembered.

Be useful before you sell

Standing out is not about performing. It is about showing you paid attention. Mike likes short personalized video for this. Forty seconds to a minute, made for one person, showing you did your homework on their situation. It works because it is rare. Most outreach is generic. When a buyer sees you understand their problem before you ask for anything, trust shows up at the very first touch. That is what makes you stand out.

Standing out is a habit, not a stunt

One clever moment fades fast. Mike's view is that standing out comes from doing the clear, useful thing over and over until people associate your name with a specific problem you solve. It is a mindset more than a tactic. Keep it simple, keep it consistent, and let the reputation build.
