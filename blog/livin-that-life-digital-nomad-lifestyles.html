TLDR

  • The travel came after the systems, not before.
  • Process, delegation, and client fit are what make it work on the road.

Livin That Life is Riley Bennett's show about people who earn while they travel. I came on to share how I run my business from the road. In 2022 I worked from 38 cities across 10 countries. Here is what made that possible.

The freedom came from the boring stuff

People assume the travel comes first. For me it came last. First I had to fix the business so it did not need me in one place. That meant a clear process, work I could hand off, and clients who were a good fit. None of that is glamorous. But once it was in place, my location stopped mattering. I could open a laptop in a new city and the business kept running.

How the income keeps coming

I do not run ads to keep leads flowing. I lean on organic reach, referrals, personalized video, and guesting on shows like this one. Then I keep my own time low by delegating the repeatable work and holding on to the few things only I should do. That mix is what lets the money keep coming while I move around. I go deeper on the how on Digital Nomad Stories.
