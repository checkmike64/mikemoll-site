TLDR

  • Good marketing is simple: be clear, be useful, and follow up.
  • You do not need ads to bring in the right clients.
  • A clear offer does more marketing work than a clever campaign.

Mike joined Frits Bussemaker and Ashutosh Garg on The Brand Called You to break down the ABC of marketing. His version is not about big budgets or clever campaigns. It is about the simple stuff that brings in clients and makes money.

Here is how Mike keeps marketing lean.

Start with a clear offer

Before any tactic, Mike looks at the offer. Most marketing struggles because the thing being sold is fuzzy. If your offer points at one clear outcome for one clear buyer, half the marketing job is already done. The message writes itself, and the right person recognizes it fast. A clever campaign cannot save a confusing offer, so fix the offer first.

Be useful where your buyer already is

Mike does not chase every channel. He shows up where his buyers already spend time and tries to be useful. That means organic content, referrals, short personalized video, and guesting on podcasts. Each one does the same job. Be helpful, build a little trust, and let that trust do the selling. It is lean by design, and it brings in warmer leads than ads ever will.

Follow up like it matters

The step most people skip is the follow-up. Mike's view is that if your work fixes a future pain for a good-fit buyer, moving them forward is your job, not a bother. People buy when they feel understood, so a simple, human follow-up beats a slick funnel. Marketing that makes money is often just the basics done consistently.
