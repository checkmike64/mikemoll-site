What podcasting does for your sales

Hosts send you business, listeners buy, and the people who book a call already trust you.

    Podcasts hold attention better than any other channel: listeners finish about 80% of an episode. Buyers want about 43 minutes with your content before they buy, and one episode can cover it in a single sitting.

    Each episode gives you three to seven short clips and an email to your list, and you never set up a camera. Send a clip to a prospect before a sales call and they show up warmer.

Frequently asked questions

Whether you're new to podcast guesting or you've tried it on your own, these are the questions I hear most.

Is this right for me?

It fits established service founders whose offer already sells: consultants, coaches, agency owners and fractional experts. You want the credibility of a personal brand without filming yourself every day. It isn't a fit if you don't have paying clients yet.

Is the $100K guaranteed?

No. The $100K is what the right founder, on the right shows, can build to over time through hosts, listeners and content. It depends on your offer and your follow-through. I've traced $386,000 to my own appearances.

What is the guarantee?

It's the Fit-First Promise. I only accept founders I know hosts will want, and we check that on your free assessment. If our 2.5-hour launch call doesn't give you an offer you're proud to pitch, tell me before our first weekly call and you get a full refund.

Do I have to become a content creator?

No. You show up for the conversation, and the host records and publishes it. Each episode gives you clips and an email you can share, and a simple plan lets your team handle that part.

Do you provide a VA?

No. The podcast package is simple enough for your own assistant to run. If you'd rather my team books the shows for you, that's our done-for-you option, and it costs more.

How much time does it take?

Most of your time goes into the launch call and the first few weeks. Your first pitches go out within days, and we aim to land your first interviews within 30 days. After that, your part is the interviews and the chats with hosts afterward.

What if I'm not confident doing interviews?

I'm an introvert too. You'll prepare your stories in three lengths and use the two sticky notes I keep by my camera. Preparation does most of the work.
