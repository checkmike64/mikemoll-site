Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Impact Leads LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit mikemoll.co (the "Site") or interact with our content, forms, and services. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

The Site is operated by Impact Leads LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, you can reach us at mike@mikemoll.co or by mail at the address listed at the end of this page.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you fill out a form, sign up for a free resource, register for a workshop, request information, or purchase something, we collect the details you provide. This can include your name, email address, and any other information you choose to share in a form or message.

Payment information

When you buy something through the Site, payment is processed by our third-party payment processor, Stripe. Your full payment card details are entered directly with Stripe and are handled under Stripe's own security and privacy standards. We do not store your full card number on our systems.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically collect certain technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages, and how you interact with the Site. We collect this through cookies and similar technologies, including analytics tools such as Google Analytics (deployed via Google Tag Manager).

Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the Site function and help us understand how visitors use it. We use them for basic Site operation, analytics, and to measure the performance of our content and marketing. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may not work as intended if you do.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the resources, trainings, workshops, or services you request.
  • To process payments and manage your purchases.
  • To send you emails, including content, updates, and marketing messages (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To understand how the Site is used and to improve our content and offers.
  • To protect the Site, prevent fraud, and comply with our legal obligations.

Email and marketing

If you opt in, we may send you marketing emails through our customer relationship and email platform. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. You may still receive non-marketing messages related to a purchase or request you made.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Site and our business, and only as needed for them to perform their work. These include:

  • GoHighLevel — our customer relationship management (CRM) and email platform.
  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Google — website analytics and tag management.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our agreements, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services, maintain our records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of certain uses. To make a request, email us at mike@mikemoll.co. We will respond consistent with applicable law. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any message.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party links

The Site may link to other websites or services we do not control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
