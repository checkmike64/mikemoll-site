Online Networking and Lead Generation
In this episode, Mike Moll and I discuss how to effectively and successfully generate leads. We also discuss how to make connections in the world of online networking. You will learn how to make better and long lasting connections, and the importance of connecting with leads for long-term clients.

The Importance of Following Up with Online Networking
Not everyone makes a decision right away on whether or not they want to try a product or service. If you have a maybe as an answer, be sure to follow up. Answer any questions they may have.

Social Media Musts with Online Networking
- Set it up to look credible
- Put out high quality content
- Don't fixate on followers or likes
- Post for your IDEAL followers
- 4-6 posts a week
- VIDEO is a must for credibility
- Address questions/problems in your content
- Content isn't to get you leads

BEST TRAFFIC:
Now you have to send traffic to the content.
- Co content making experiences with complimentary businesses
- Partnership reach outs
- Podcast interviews
- When going for partnerships, ask: does it bring value to my audience that I can't provide? Is it a direct competitor?

How to Stand Out in Your Outreach
- Check the person's profile
- Send video or audio messages (consider 150 a week on LinkedIn; text is easy to ignore)
- Follow them
- Message example: Hi it's ___. Love xyz. Just wanted to say hi. Second: invite to FB group, collab, invite or feedback. Consider what should be put on profile.

How To Get Your Audience to Want to Hear What You Have to Say
- Consider the numbers and the Rule of 10
- Realize that "no" is a part of the game, so don't get discouraged
- If someone isn't interested, ask them if they know someone who would be
- Cold selling direct messages where you are just upfront selling does not work
- Consistency is key. Mike produces anywhere between 150-250 videos per week

The Rule of 10
Usually 10% should be the goal for responses from your outreach. You should be able to get 10% of the 10% to say yes to your offer. If you reach out to 100 people, you should be receiving around 10 responses. From the 10 responses you receive, one should be a yes. Don't be discouraged by the no's. A no is even an opportunity. They may know someone who can benefit from your products or services.
